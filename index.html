How to read the prices

Target menu price is the drink's ingredient cost divided by the target cost percentage.

Target costCalculationMeaning
20%Drink cost ÷ 0.20Higher selling price
25%Drink cost ÷ 0.25Middle target
30%Drink cost ÷ 0.30Lower selling price

Suggested prices are shown both as the exact calculated amount and rounded up to the next $0.25. Rounded prices are a convenience, not a required menu price.
